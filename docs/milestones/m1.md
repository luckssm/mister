# Milestone 1: "A match happens"

The walking skeleton: end to end, with gameplay intentionally crude;
milestone 2 makes it believable. Tasks are roughly in
dependency order, one PR each.

## Tasks

1. **Seeded RNG:** deterministic sequence per seed. Test: same seed, same sequence.
2. **Pitch grid:** parametric lanes × bands with a default; `Zone` type and adjacency.
3. **Minimal domain:** `Player` with a small attribute subset (passing,
   shooting, defending, goalkeeping, maybe pace), `Team`, one fixed formation
   mapping players to home zones. The full attribute model is designed
   properly; M1 uses only a subset.
4. **Coverage:** `coverage(zone, team, grid)`, which says who defends a zone.
5. **Match state and resumable step loop:** clock, score, ball (team,
   carrier, zone), step by step, with halftime as a pause point. Likely to split.
6. **Action contract and a placeholder `chooseAction`:** pass unless in the
   final band, then shoot.
7. **`resolvePass`:** success odds from passer vs covering defenders; a
   failure is a turnover. Likely to split.
8. **`resolveShot` and a simple xG formula.**
9. **Event log and `applyOutcome`:** every step emits events.
10. **`deriveStats`:** score, shots, xG, possession %, passes completed, all
    derived from the log.
11. **Fixture data:** two fictional teams in JSON plus the loader adapter.
12. **CLI:** `pnpm sim --home A --away B --seed 42`.
13. **Match invariants and a loose realism smoke test:** score equals goal
    events; time only moves forward; over 1,000 matches the average total
    goals is between 1 and 5, and fewer than 1% of matches have 8+ goals.

## Not in M1

Dribbles, tackles as a separate action, crosses, fatigue, tactics, set
pieces, súmula prose, risk report.

## Definition of done

- `pnpm sim` prints a full match event log, the final score and basic stats
- The same seed always produces the same match
- Over 1,000 simulated matches, the average total goals per match is between
  1 and 5, and fewer than 1% of matches have 8 or more total goals (task 13)
